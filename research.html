Research overview

Boyi Fu's research interests center on spatial intelligence, 3D object reconstruction, the embodied world, and AI4AI. He is interested in learning representations of objects and scenes that connect visual perception with spatial reasoning, and in how this understanding can support interaction with the physical world.

付博亦的研究興趣集中於空間智能、三維物體重建、具身世界與 AI4AI。他關注如何學習物體與場景的表徵，連接視覺感知與空間推理，並以三維理解支持智能體與物理世界的互動。
